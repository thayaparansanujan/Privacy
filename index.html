Privacy Policy

Jaffna Ride · Effective date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jaffna Ride ("we", "our" or "us") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Jaffna Ride mobile application (the "App"). Please read this policy before using the App.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: information you provide when registering or using the App, such as your name, phone number and account details.
  • Location information: with your device permission, the App may collect your precise location to show your position, support vehicle availability, help connect drivers and customers, and provide ride-related services. Location may be collected while the App is in use. If the App requests and you grant background-location permission, location may also be collected while the App is in the background for the relevant availability or ride feature. You can manage location permission in your device settings; disabling it may affect location-based features.
  • Ride and vehicle information: information you submit about bookings, trips, vehicle details, availability and service activity.
  • Device and usage information: technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, diagnostic data and app interactions, where made available to us.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide ride and vehicle-availability features, display relevant location information, coordinate customers and drivers, communicate about service activity, maintain app security, troubleshoot problems and improve the App.

3. Location permissions and controls

Location access is used only for location-based functions of the App. You can review or change the App's location permissions at any time in your device settings. If background location is enabled in the App, we will explain the feature's purpose in the App and request the relevant device permission. You may turn off location access, although some features may then be unavailable or less accurate.

4. When information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information:

  • with customers or drivers when needed to provide a ride or vehicle-availability service, including relevant trip and location details;
  • with service providers that help us host, maintain, secure or operate the App, subject to appropriate safeguards;
  • when required by applicable law, a lawful request or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public; or
  • with your direction or consent.

5. Data storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain service records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details, unless we are required to retain it by law.

To request access to, correction of or deletion of your account information, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate service and security purposes.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

8. Third-party services

The App may rely on third-party services for functions such as hosting, maps, notifications or analytics. Those providers may process information as needed to deliver their services and are governed by their own privacy terms. The services actually used by the released App should be identified in the final version of this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the App after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.
